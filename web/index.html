Channels

FM for narrowband voice (public safety, weather, marine, FRS/GMRS). AM for airband. WBFM for broadcast radio. A locked-out channel stays in the list but the scan skips it.

    Plays in the browser as a normal MP3 stream. You can also open /stream.mp3 in VLC or any player.

    Settings

    Open while the level is at or above this. Watch the meter on an empty channel and set it a few dB above the noise. The line on the meter shows where it sits. Loud static on an empty FM channel will hold it open; use Level swing for that.

    Open while the level moves by at least this much over a second. Built for FM quieting, where a real signal is quieter than open static but more dynamic. Start at 3.

    rtl_fm's own squelch (-l). It sends nothing at all while the carrier is below the level, so dead air is dead silent. Raise it from 0 until an empty channel stops producing audio. Changing it restarts rtl_fm.

    Dwell is how long a channel has to stay quiet before the scan moves on. Long enough to catch the reply, usually 2 to 4.

    16000 Hz is plenty for voice, use 32000 or more for WBFM. Gain is auto or a number like 40. PPM is your dongle's frequency error, rtl_test -p measures it. Device is an index or serial, only needed with more than one dongle. Changing any of these restarts rtl_fm, and a rate change reconnects listeners.

    Changing it logs out every other session.
